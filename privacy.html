Privacy Policy

Last updated 12 August 2026

The short version

Menoua records your voice so you can hear yourself change over time. Those recordings stay on your phone. We do not upload them, listen to them, or use them to train anything. We store your progress so it survives a new phone, and you can delete all of it from inside the app in two taps.

1. Who we are

Menoua ("we", "us") makes a pronunciation training app. For the purposes of the UK GDPR and the EU GDPR we are the data controller for the information described below.

Contact: privacy@menoua.app

2. Your voice recordings

This is the part that matters most, so it comes first.

  • Menoua asks for microphone access so you can record yourself and compare takes.
  • Recordings are written to your device's private app storage.
  • They are not uploaded to our servers. No human at Menoua can hear them.
  • They are not used to train speech models, ours or anyone else's.
  • Deleting the app deletes them. You can also clear them from Settings.

What we do store is a small numeric summary of a recording's loudness over time — the shape you see drawn as a waveform. It cannot be turned back into audio and contains no speech.

Health information

During setup we ask three optional questions about cardiovascular conditions, pregnancy and epilepsy. The only thing we do with the answers is switch breathing exercises to a gentler version. You may skip them, and skipping costs you nothing but the breath-hold variant. This is special-category data under GDPR and we process it only with your explicit consent. You can clear it at any time in Settings.

Menoua is not a medical device and does not diagnose anything.

4. Children

Menoua is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has given us data, write to privacy@menoua.app and we will delete it.

6. How long we keep it

Progress data is kept while your account exists. Delete your account and it is removed within 30 days, other than anything we must retain for tax or fraud purposes. Crash reports are kept for 90 days.

7. Your rights

You can ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, restrict how we use it, or object to it. Where processing rests on consent you can withdraw that consent at any time.

Deleting everything

Open the app, go to You → Delete my account. It removes your account and all associated data. You do not need to email us and we will not try to talk you out of it. Do not have the app installed? Use our web deletion page instead.

If you think we have handled your data badly you can complain to your local supervisory authority. In France that is the CNIL.

8. Notifications

Menoua asks for notification permission once, at the end of your first session, and only so it can tell you when your overnight result is ready. Saying no changes nothing else about the app. You can turn them off in your phone's settings at any time.

9. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app before the change takes effect.
