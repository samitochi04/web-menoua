If you never added an email

Most Menoua accounts start this way, so there is nothing on our side yet to send a code to. Tell us where to confirm once it is done, and anything that helps us find the right account. We remove it by hand.

What is deleted, and what we keep

Profile, progress, session history, listening test results Deleted immediately (in-app or verified web) or within 30 days (unverified web request).
Voice recordings Never stored on our servers to begin with — they live in the app's private storage on your device and are removed the moment your device deletes them.
Crash and diagnostic logs Kept up to 90 days for debugging, then deleted automatically. Not linked to your name.
Purchase records Retained by Apple, Google or Stripe as required by tax law, independent of your Menoua account. We do not control this retention.
